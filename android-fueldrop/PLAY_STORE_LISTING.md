# FillMeNow — Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App details

**App name**  
FillMeNow

**Category**  
Auto & Vehicles

**Short description**  
Find the fuel stop that saves you money after travel, not just at the pump.

**Full description**

FillMeNow helps you find the fuel stop that is actually worth the trip.

Instead of ranking stations only by the number on the fuel sign, FillMeNow can compare nearby reported fuel prices with your estimated travel cost, search radius and vehicle settings.

### Find better-value fuel
- Compare nearby fuel stations on a live map.
- See the best-value stop for your selected area.
- Compare pump price, distance and estimated trip-adjusted value.
- Open navigation to a selected station.
- Save regular fuel stops for quick access.

### Built around your vehicle
Set your vehicle type, typical fill size and fuel consumption so FillMeNow can make more useful comparisons.

### Fuel-price alerts
Choose optional price alerts for your selected area and fuel type. Notifications can be turned off at any time.

### Meet Dash
Dash is FillMeNow's mascot and guide, appearing around the app to help explain features without getting in the way.

### Current launch coverage
FillMeNow currently supports:
- New South Wales
- Western Australia
- Tasmania

Fuel data sources include NSW Government FuelCheck and FuelWatch, Government of Western Australia.

Fuel prices, travel times, distances and estimated savings can change. Always confirm the displayed pump price before purchase and use navigation safely.
