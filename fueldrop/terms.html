FuelDrop Terms & Disclaimer

Effective: 5 October 2026

FuelDrop is a fuel-price comparison and trip-value utility. By using FuelDrop, you acknowledge the following.

Fuel prices

Fuel prices are sourced from official or public fuel-price feeds where available. Prices can change after they are reported. Always confirm the displayed pump price at the station before purchasing fuel.

Estimated savings, distance and travel time

FuelDrop may show estimated travel distance, travel time, trip fuel cost and potential savings. These are estimates only and may differ because of traffic, road conditions, detours, vehicle use, fuel consumption, map data or price changes.

Navigation and driving safety

Navigation links may open a third-party mapping or navigation service. Do not interact with FuelDrop in a way that distracts you while driving. Follow road rules, signage, closures and safe-driving requirements.

Availability

FuelDrop may change, suspend or remove features, fuel providers, stations or geographic coverage. Data feeds and third-party services can be unavailable or delayed.

No guarantee of lowest possible price

FuelDrop is designed to help compare available reported prices and estimated trip value. It does not guarantee that a recommended station is the absolute cheapest option at the time you arrive.

Third-party services

FuelDrop relies on third-party hosting, maps, notification infrastructure and fuel-data providers. Their availability and terms are outside FuelDrop's control.
