## Description

FillMeNow helps you compare nearby fuel stations and choose the stop that offers the best estimated value for your trip.

Instead of looking only at the number on the fuel sign, FillMeNow can combine reported pump price with distance, estimated travel fuel cost and your vehicle settings.

### Find fuel around you
- View nearby stations on a native interactive map.
- See all available stations inside your selected radius.
- Zoom into grouped stations in busy areas.
- Compare reported fuel prices and distance.
- Search a suburb or postcode or use your current location.

### Find the Best Stop
FillMeNow ranks stations using estimated total trip value, helping you see whether a cheaper pump price is still worth the drive.

### Set up your vehicle
Choose a vehicle preset or enter your own typical fill size and fuel economy.

### Save regular stations
Keep the stations you use most in a native saved-stations list.

### Navigate
Open your selected station directly in Apple Maps for driving directions.

### Launch coverage
FillMeNow currently supports:
- New South Wales
- Western Australia
- Tasmania

Fuel data includes official government fuel-price sources such as NSW Government FuelCheck and FuelWatch, Government of Western Australia.

Fuel prices can change after they are reported. Distances, travel times and savings are estimates. Always confirm the displayed pump price before purchasing fuel and follow safe-driving practices.
