FillMeNow Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 October 2026

FillMeNow helps users compare nearby fuel stations and estimated trip value. This policy explains the information FillMeNow accesses, how it is used, and the controls available to you.

Location: FillMeNow only requests device location when you choose a location feature such as “Use current location”. FillMeNow does not require background location access.

Information FillMeNow may access or collect

  • Location data: latitude and longitude, and an area label such as suburb, postcode or state, when you choose to use your location or select an area.
  • Fuel and vehicle preferences: fuel type, search radius, typical fill size, fuel economy and vehicle type.
  • Saved stations: stations you choose to save. These are stored locally on your device/browser in the current version.
  • Notification information: when you enable alerts, FillMeNow stores the browser/device push subscription plus the location and alert settings needed to send those alerts.
  • Technical information: hosting, mapping and backend services may process normal network information such as IP address, browser/device information and service logs required to deliver and secure the service.

How information is used

FillMeNow uses this information to show nearby stations, calculate estimated trip value, remember your preferences, provide navigation links, send alerts you enable, secure the service and diagnose technical problems.

Location permissions

FillMeNow uses location for the user-facing feature of finding fuel near you. Location is requested in the foreground when you initiate a location action. FillMeNow does not intentionally request continuous background location tracking.

Push notifications

If you enable alerts, FillMeNow stores a push subscription and the settings needed to determine which fuel-price notification to send. Turning alerts off removes the active push subscription. Using “Reset app data” also attempts to unsubscribe the device before clearing local settings.

Nearby filters and truck settings

Truck dimensions, vehicle configuration, Nearby filters and your offer-eligibility confirmations are stored on this device. They are not automatically attached to support reports or sent to a truck-routing service. Offers are shown only from a reviewed station-specific catalogue. Standard navigation does not apply truck dimensions.

Queensland area searches may send the suburb or postcode you type to the Queensland Government boundary service. These results are approximate area centres, not verified street addresses or station locations. Boundary data: © State of Queensland.

Local storage

FillMeNow stores preferences, the selected area and saved stations in browser storage so the app can remember your choices. You can clear this information using “Reset app data” in FillMeNow or by clearing site/app data in your browser or device settings.

Service providers and data sources

FillMeNow uses service providers needed to operate the app, including hosting, backend/database infrastructure, mapping, browser push notification infrastructure and official fuel-data services. Fuel-price data currently includes NSW Government FuelCheck for New South Wales and Tasmania, FuelWatch, Government of Western Australia, for Western Australia, and the approved Fuel Prices QLD feed when connected.

Sharing and sale of data

FillMeNow does not use location data for advertising. Information is disclosed to service providers only as needed to operate the functions described above or where required by law. FillMeNow does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you reset FillMeNow, clear site/app data or remove it. Push subscription data is retained while alerts are enabled and is removed when the subscription is deleted or unsubscribed. Operational logs may be retained for reasonable security, reliability and troubleshooting periods.

Security

FillMeNow uses HTTPS for data in transit and limits public access to backend data. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

FillMeNow is a general-audience fuel utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Your choices

  • Use a manually selected area instead of device location.
  • Deny or revoke location permission in your browser or device settings.
  • Turn notifications off at any time.
  • Remove saved stations or reset FillMeNow data.
  • Contact FillMeNow regarding privacy or deletion questions.

Help chat and support reports

Ask Dash uses built-in app guidance. Questions typed into this helper are kept only in the current page and are not sent to a chatbot provider. When you explicitly submit a report, the report text, selected category, app version, browser family, screen size and current app screen are stored privately for investigation. A random device token is stored locally; only its hash is stored with the report. Do not include names, email addresses, passwords or exact addresses. Reports do not attach GPS coordinates or saved stations.

Reports are retained for up to 90 days. You can delete your last report from the same device using the support panel. Weekly investigation tasks may be added to the repository after removing personal information; private report text is not automatically published. Resetting local app data does not itself delete server reports and removes this device’s report-deletion token.

Contact

Privacy enquiries: Ask Dash / send a private support report

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when FillMeNow features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Optional destinations

Your optional destination (fmnJourney) is saved on this device and can be cleared in the location panel or by resetting FillMeNow. Destination searches use the existing area lookup service. Your destination and chosen station are passed to Google Maps only when you choose directions via that station. They are not automatically attached to support reports. Location is requested only when you select Use current location; you may refuse permission and search manually.

Live location and settings

My location and Follow me request GPS only after you tap them and grant permission. Following stops when you switch away from the app. No location trail is stored; the latest search point is used to request nearby fuel data. Panning pauses camera following. Vehicle settings and unfinished field drafts stay in this browser or installed app, not in a shared account. A destination direction filter uses approximate geometry, not a road route or clearance guarantee.
